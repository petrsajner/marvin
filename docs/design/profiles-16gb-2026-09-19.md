# What a 16 GB card can actually run

19 September 2026. Measured on the owner's 5090 under an explicit budget, which
is how the earlier qualifications were done too: capacity transfers, speed does
not. The card this is for is a **4060 Ti 16 GB**.

## What it was offered before

| model | profile | context | measured |
|---|---|---|---|
| IQ3_S | Q8 · 64k | 64k | 13.617 GB |
| IQ3_S | Q8 · 48k | 48k | 13.008 GB |

Two entries, one model, the weakest quant, and in both the vision projector had
to sit on the processor - so looking at a screenshot was slow. Nothing else fit.

## The band, not the budget

Profiles are matched by card class, not by comparing a figure to a budget, and
the approved 16-class entries occupy **13.0 to 13.6 GB**. That gap below the
nominal 16 GB is the room the desktop needs; it is not spare capacity. Candidates
measured at 15.0-15.5 GB were therefore rejected even though they "fit 16 GB" on
a machine whose desktop was already accounted for. On a 4060 Ti with a browser
open they would fail to load.

This was learned the hard way in this session: the first round of candidates was
chosen against 16.0 GB and every one of them would have been wrong.

## Measured

All on IQ3_S or Q2_K_XL, keys and values as stated, and the figure is what the
server allocated with the desktop's own usage subtracted.

| candidate | VRAM | prompt read | generate |
|---|---|---|---|
| Q2 · 64k · vision on GPU | **12.96** | 2915 | 96 |
| Q2 · 128k, K q8 / V q4 | 13.28 | 2986 | 93 |
| Q2 · 192k, K q4 / V q4 | 13.73 | 2981 | 96 |
| IQ3_S · 128k, K q4 / V q4 | 14.05 | 3101 | 88 |
| IQ3_S · 96k, K q8 / V q4 | 14.08 | 3092 | 85 |
| Q2 · 96k · vision on GPU | 14.19 | 2924 | 95 |
| Q2 · 128k | 14.31 | 2787 | 88 |
| Q2 · 128k, K q8/V q4 · vision on GPU | 14.42 | 2979 | 95 |
| Q2 · 192k, K q8 / V q4 | 15.13 | 2966 | 96 |
| IQ3_S · 192k, K q4 / V q4 | 15.48 | 3043 | 86 |
| Q2 · 128k · vision on GPU | 15.40 | 2934 | 96 |
| IQ3_S · 128k (full Q8 cache) | 16.05 | 2850 | 85 |

Three things this says:

1. **The cache type costs nothing in speed.** Across every combination, reading
   stayed between 2787 and 3101 tokens a second and generating between 85 and 96.
   The only price of a smaller cache is recall, which no number here measures.
2. **Quantising only the values is worth a gigabyte.** IQ3_S at 128k needs
   16.05 GB with a full Q8 cache and 15.04 GB with Q8 keys and Q4 values. Values
   tolerate quantisation far better than keys, so this is the cheap half.
3. **Two bits buys the projector back.** Q2_K_XL is 2 GB smaller than IQ3_S,
   which is enough to keep vision on the graphics card *and* have 96k of context -
   something a 16 GB card could not have at all before.

Q2 also generated slightly faster than IQ3_S (95 against 85), which follows from
reading fewer bytes of weights per token.

## Approved

| model | profile | context | measured | for |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| Q2_K_XL | Q8 · 64k · vision on GPU | 64k | 12.96 | screenshots, safest |
| Q2_K_XL | Q8 · 96k · vision on GPU | 96k | 14.19 | screenshots with room |
| Q2_K_XL | Q8 · 128k | 128k | 14.31 | most context, full cache |
| Q2_K_XL | Q4 · 192k | 192k | 13.73 | most context of all |
| IQ3_S | Q8/Q4 cache · 96k | 96k | 14.08 | better weights than Q2 |
| IQ3_S | Q4 · 128k | 128k | 14.05 | better weights, more room |

Eight entries for a 16 GB card instead of two, spanning the real trade-off:
weight quality against context against whether the projector is on the card.

## Two decisions made deliberately

**Q2 is offered but never chosen automatically.** It is marked
`optional_download`, which already meant "do not fetch unless asked" and now also
means "do not select unless asked". Automatic selection must not quietly trade
weight quality for context: a two-bit 27B model is a real drop, worst on code and
on following instructions exactly, and that is the owner's trade to make.

**A reduced value cache is never the automatic default either.** `best_fit` now
prefers a profile whose keys and values match, so a 16 GB card still starts on
IQ3_S at 64k with a full cache. The quantised-cache entries are there to be
chosen.

## What was not added, and why

`K q8 / V q4` was also measured on a 24 GB budget, since the same saving should
open up a bigger context or a better quant there:

| candidate | VRAM | against the approved maximum of 21.92 |
|---|---|---|
| Q4 · 192k, K q8/V q4, vision on GPU | 22.05 | +0.13 |
| Q5 · 96k, K q8/V q4, vision on GPU | 22.24 | +0.32 |
| Q5 · 128k, K q8/V q4, vision on GPU | 23.19 | +1.27 |
| Q5 · 192k, K q4/V q4, vision on GPU | 23.64 | +1.72 |
| Q4 · 256k, K q8/V q4, vision on GPU | 23.99 | +2.07 |
| Q5 · 192k, K q8/V q4, vision on GPU | 25.13 | +3.21 |

The first is tempting - twice the context of the current 24 GB offer at the same
weights - and it sits within the noise of the approved maximum. It was still left
out: neither of the owner's machines has a 24 GB card, so it could not be tried
where it matters, and the margin it eats exists for the desktop. If such a card
appears, this is the first thing to measure on it.

## What these numbers do not say

Speed was measured on a 5090. The card this is for is a **4060 Ti 16 GB**: a
128-bit bus at roughly 288 GB/s against the 5090's ~1800, on a PCIe 4.0 x8 link.
Generation for a dense 27B model follows memory bandwidth closely, so expect a
fraction of the 85-96 tokens a second recorded here, and prompt reading
proportionally slower. That is reasoning from the hardware, not a measurement.

The same reasoning is why **Nemotron 30B with experts on the processor was not
added for 16 GB** even though it fits at 13.8 GB: measured at 476-610 tokens a
second reading here, it depends on moving expert weights across the link on every
token, and that link is half as wide on this card. It would be the slowest option
on the very card it was meant to serve.

And no number here measures what a Q2 quant or a 4-bit key cache costs in
answers. That shows up as a worse patch or a forgotten detail from twenty steps
back, and only use will tell.
